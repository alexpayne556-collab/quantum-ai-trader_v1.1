# PART 1: THE MENTAL MODELS
## How to Think Like Someone Who Predicts The Future

### MODEL #1: THE CAUSATION CHAIN
Think backwards from the stock move to its cause.

Every 2-3x stock move has a cause that appears 6-12 months BEFORE the move.

**Example: SCCO Copper Shortage (CONCEPTUAL EXAMPLE - NOT A RECOMMENDATION)**
```
Month 1-3: Chilean mine delayed (specialists know, nobody else notices)
Month 4-6: Global supply reports show tightening (traders notice)
Month 7-9: Commodity price breaks to new high (institutions start buying)
Month 10-12: Company reports +40% margin expansion (retail finally buys)

The CAUSE = mine delay (month 1-3)
The EFFECT = stock 2x (month 10-12)
The LAG = 9 months
```

**Your Job:** See month 1-3 information and predict month 10-12 effect.

**How:** Ask "If stock 2-3x happens, what HAD to be true 6-12 months ago?"
- Company needs +50-100% earnings growth
- Earnings growth requires margin expansion or volume growth
- Margin expansion requires supply shortage or cost advantage
- Supply shortage requires supply gap (predictable)
- Supply gap appears in month 1-3 (public data)

Chain complete. Prediction made.

### MODEL #2: INVERSION FRAMEWORK
Work backwards from price target.

**Formula:**
```
IF stock at $150 (3x from $50)
THEN EPS must be: $150/20 P/E = $7.50 (vs $3 today = +150% growth)

Does this require:
a) Margin expansion only? (from 30% to 45% = +500 basis points)
b) Volume growth only? (2x production = NOT possible in 12-24 months)
c) Both? (Margins +300bps + volume +30% = POSSIBLE)

Which requires:
- Supply constraint (shortage) = YES, it's developing
- Cost advantage = YES, company has it
- No new competition = YES, 5+ year supply/demand gap

Conclusion: Math works. Thesis is viable.
```

**Never buy a stock without doing this math backwards.**

### MODEL #3: TIMING GAP HIERARCHY
There's a lag between knowledge and price.

```
Level 1: Specialists know (mining engineers, policy experts)
         └─ Timeline: Public data exists, most ignore

Level 2: Industry professionals know (traders, analysts)
         └─ Timeline: Following the space, 3-6 months behind Level 1

Level 3: Financial professionals know (sell-side analysts starting research)
         └─ Timeline: Publishing first notes, 3-6 months behind Level 2

Level 4: Institutions start knowing (fund managers reading analyst notes)
         └─ Timeline: Building first positions, 2-4 months behind Level 3

Level 5: Media discovers it (Bloomberg, CNBC, mainstream coverage)
         └─ Timeline: Major story breaks, 1-3 months behind Level 4

Level 6: Retail discovers it (everyone on Reddit/Twitter talking about it)
         └─ Timeline: FOMO buying, final wave of move

Level 7: Stock fully priced in (move is over)
         └─ Timeline: Time to exit or short

Total lag: 12-18 months from Level 1 to Level 7
```

**Your Positioning:**
- Enter: Level 2-3 (6-12 months before retail)
- Increase: Level 3-4 (when institution starts noticing)
- Sell 25%: Level 4 (first profit taking)
- Sell 50%: Level 5 (media coverage, analyst upgrades)
- Sell 75%: Level 5-6 (institutional wave + retail arriving)
- Keep 25%: Let it run to Level 7
