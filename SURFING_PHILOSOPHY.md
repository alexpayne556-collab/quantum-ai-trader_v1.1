## 🌊 THE OCEAN METAPHOR (Extended)

**The market is an ocean.**

We're not just looking for waves that are already breaking.  
We're looking for **ripples** and **formations** that suggest a wave is coming.

**Small ripples** (penny stocks) = Too fast, crash in hours  
**Big waves** (small/mid-caps) = Last 2-3 days minimum  
**Mega waves** (large caps) = Last weeks but harder to catch early

**Examples of real waves that rode for days:**
- KDK (Kodiak Sciences)
- MVST (Microvast Holdings)
- UUUU (Energy Fuels)

**Pattern observed:** They ride for a few days, usually come back (mean reversion).

**Key insight:** Riding ONE wave can be safe after enough testing.  
You don't have to jump off every time it dips. Stay on the board through pullbacks.

### 2. Wave Entry (Catch It Early, Day 1)
**Traditional Approach:** Wait for pullback, perfect entry, confirmation of confirmation  
**Surfing Approach:** Enter Day 1 when wave confirmed (will ride 2-3 days minimum)

**How:**
- Wave confirmed = sector shows 3+ stocks moving together, volume 2-5x
- Entry time: 10:00-10:30 AM Day 1 (after initial volatility)

### 3. Wave Riding (Stay On Board - Days 1-3)
**Traditional Approach:** Set target, hold, hope it hits  
**Surfing Approach:** Ride for 2-3 days, stay through pullbacks, trust the wave

**How (Multi-day momentum):**
- Day 1: Entry (10 AM-12 PM), monitor sector strength into close
- Day 2: Hold position, watch for sector continuation or reversal
- Day 3+: Exit window opens (can sell without PDT penalty)

**Stay signals (keep riding):**
- Sector still green overall (other stocks still moving)
- Stock still above Day 1 entry (wave hasn't crashed)
- Volume still elevated (2x+ average)
- No major reversal pattern (top formation, volume spike down)

**User observation validated:**
- "They ride for a few days" (KDK, MVST, UUUU)
- "Usually come back" (mean reversion = don't panic sell on Day 2 dip)
- "Riding one wave can be safe" (hold through pullbacks, trust the pattern)

**Why:** PDT rules force us to ride waves properly (2-3 days). This is GOOD - catches full move, avoids overtrading.

### 4. Wave Exit (Day 3-5, Before Full Crash)
**Traditional Approach:** Hold for maximum gain, ride it until stop loss  
**Surfing Approach:** Exit Day 3-5 while still green, before wave fully crashes

**How (Exit criteria):**
- **Time-based:** Day 3-5 (PDT safe, wave likely losing steam)
- **Profit target:** 10-20% gain (realistic for 2-3 day hold)
- **Sector reversal:** Other stocks in sector turn red, leaders fade
- **Trailing stop:** 5-7% from peak (lock in gains)
- **Pattern reversal:** Top formation, distribution volume

**PDT management:**
- Entry Day 1 = Trade #1
- Exit Day 3+ = Trade #2 (safe, no PDT penalty)
- If strong: Can hold to Day 4-5, but watch for reversal

**User insight - "Usually come back":**
- After wave crashes, stock often mean reverts
- Don't FOMO back in immediately (wait for next wave formation)
- Cash is a position (wait for next clean ripple/wave)

**Why:** Exit at +15% Day 3 beats holding for +25% and crashing to +5% Day 7.
