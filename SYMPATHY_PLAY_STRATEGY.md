## The "Hunting" Algorithm (How to Find Them)

To catch these, scan for **three specific signals** that appear *before* the news hits the retail feed.

### 📡 Signal A: The "Volume Pre-Shock"
*   **What it is:** Volume spikes >200% of average, but price has NOT moved yet.
*   **What it means:** Insiders or algos are positioning.
*   **Scanner Setting:**
    *   Price < $10
    *   Relative Volume > 2.0 (Current Vol vs 20-Day Avg)
    *   Price Change Today < 3% (Ideally flat or red)

### 🐳 Signal B: The "Whale Whisper" (Options Flow)
*   **What it is:** Massive "Sweep" orders in Call Options for boring stocks.
*   **What it means:** Someone knows something. (Example: MSOS Calls bought *before* the Trump news).
*   **Scanner Setting:**
    *   Unusual Options Volume > 300%
    *   Call/Put Ratio > 4.0 (Aggressive bullish betting)
    *   Implied Volatility (IV) increasing

### 📄 Signal C: The "Filing Sniper" (SEC Arbitrage)
*   **What it is:** Boring 8-K or SC 13D filings that contain "trigger words" (Redemption, Merger, Settlement).
*   **What it means:** Pure math. If a note redeems at $25 and trades at $8, it WILL go to $25. (This was ARBKL).
*   **Scanner Setting:**
    *   Real-time SEC Feed filtered for: "Redemption", "Offer", "Agreement"
