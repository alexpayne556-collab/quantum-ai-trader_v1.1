## 📊 AFTER ALL 4 RESPONSES

### Step 1: Read Everything (30 min)
- Read through all 4 responses
- Don't try to synthesize yet, just absorb
- Mark ideas that surprised you
- Note where AIs agree
- Note where AIs disagree

### Step 2: Look for Patterns (20 min)
- What shows up in multiple responses?
- What's unique to one AI?
- What's a novel idea nobody suggested before?
- What's a refinement of conventional wisdom?

### Step 3: Fill "Synthesis & Decision" Section (30 min)
In `BRAINSTORM_RESPONSES.md`:

- From Claude: [Best strategic insight]

- After all this thinking, what's ONE metric/insight you'll build around?

## 💡 QUALITY CHECK

### Is this a good response? Check:
- ✅ Proposes something I didn't think of
- ✅ Challenges an assumption I made
- ✅ Gives specific framework I could implement
- ✅ Acknowledges MY constraints (single person, free data)
- ✅ Connects multiple ideas
- ✅ Honest about what works/won't work

### Is this a weak response? Watch for:
- ❌ Generic investing advice
- ❌ Mentions technical analysis patterns
- ❌ Suggests machine learning / prediction
- ❌ Doesn't address my constraints
- ❌ Just a list with no depth
- ❌ Doesn't connect ideas

### If Response Seems Weak
- Try a follow-up: "That's good, but go deeper. What's the 2nd-order thinking here?"
- Or: "I want something more creative/novel. Can you propose something nobody's tried?"
- Or: "How does this specifically work with FREE DATA ONLY constraint?"

## 📝 TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### Tip 1: Let Them Think
- These are complex prompts
- AI systems need space to generate good responses
- Don't interrupt mid-response

### Tip 2: Capture Completely
- Copy the entire response, not just summaries
- You'll spot ideas later that you missed on first read

### Tip 3: Ask Follow-ups If Weak
- If a response seems generic, ask the AI to go deeper
- "What's something NOBODY else has thought of?"
- "How does this work given my specific constraints?"

### Tip 4: Note the Surprises
- These are ideas YOU didn't think of
- That's the whole point
- Mark them, you'll use them later

### Tip 5: Look for Conflicts
- When AIs disagree, it's interesting
- Usually one is more right for your situation
- You get to decide which
