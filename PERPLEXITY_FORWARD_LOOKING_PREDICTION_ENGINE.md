## PART 5: HOW TO BUILD THIS SYSTEM

### Step 1: Create Your Catalyst Calendar (This Week - 2 hours)

**Go to these websites:**
1. Congress.gov
2. WhiteHouse.gov
3. FDA.gov
4. SAM.gov

**For each major bill/approval with >$1B impact:**
- What is it?
- When does it pass/get approved? (likely date)
- Who benefits? (list 3-5 companies)
- What's realistic upside if it happens? (+30%? +50%? +100%?)
- What's the timeline to stock move?

**Output:** Spreadsheet with 10-15 catalysts, dates, and beneficiary companies

---

### Step 2: Identify Leading Indicators (This Week - 1 hour)

**For each catalyst:**
- What leading indicator precedes it?
- What data/news would signal it's happening?
- How many months early can you identify it?
- What should you do when you see it?

**Example:**
| Catalyst | Leading Indicator | How Early | Action |
|----------|------------------|-----------|--------|
| AI Spending Bill Passes | Bill passes committee | 2-3 months | Research beneficiaries |
| FDA Approval | Phase 3 trial results positive | 6-12 months | Start building position |
| Copper Shortage Visible | Commodity price breaks high | 6 months | Buy low-cost producers |

---

### Step 3: Set Up Monitoring (This Month - 1 week)

**Set automated alerts:**
- Yahoo Finance: Price alerts when commodities break highs
- Google Alerts: Congress.gov, FDA.gov, company news
- Email alerts: Subscribe to Congress email updates
- News alerts: Mining.com, Semiconductor Engineering, etc.

**Check daily (15 minutes):**
- Any alerts triggered?
- Any leading indicators showing?
- If yes, document and research

---

### Step 4: Research Beneficiary Companies (This Month - ongoing)

**When you identify catalyst:**
1. List all companies that benefit
2. Rank them by leverage/quality (use company research checklist)
3. Note current price and realistic target
4. Set entry price (where you'd buy)
5. Wait for leading indicator to show

**When leading indicator shows:**
- Buy at entry price you calculated
- Set stop loss at -10%
- Set target prices at +30%, +50%, +100%
- Monitor and execute profit-taking rules
