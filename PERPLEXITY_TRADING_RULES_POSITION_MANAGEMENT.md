- Set 5-10% below entry price
- Update stops after major moves up

## POSITION MANAGEMENT RULES

### Rule 1: Never Average Down More Than Once

**You Can Add To Losing Positions 1 Time:**

**Scenario:**
- C3.AI entry: $20, buy 875 shares
- Drops to $18.50: Hit stop, SELL
- Wait 1 week, if rallies back to $19.50:
  - CAN buy again at new entry
  - Do NOT buy at $18 (already stopped you out)

**Do NOT:**
- Keep buying a falling stock
- "Double down" on losing position
- Violate your stops

### Rule 2: Position Sizing After Wins

**After Taking 25% Profit, Reset Position Size:**

**Old Position: 875 shares C3 @ $20 = $17,500**
- +30% → $26
- Sell 200 shares → Get ~$5,200
- Remaining: 675 shares = $17,550

**New Position: 675 shares**
- This is now your new "full position"
- If reaches +60%: Sell 25% again (169 shares)
- Never rebuild to original size (unless it crashes 50%)

### Rule 3: Cash Management

**Keep 5% in Cash At All Times:**

**Cash Reserve Rules:**
- DON'T invest cash until opportunity is clear
- Use to buy dips (when any stock -15%)
- Use for rebalancing
- Never go to 0% cash (always have buffer)

**Dip Buying Protocol:**
- If SOUN drops 15% in 1 week: BUY
- If QCI drops 20% in 2 days: BUY
- Use cash reserve, don't sell other positions

### Rule 4: Momentum Rules

**Buy Momentum, Don't Fight It:**

**STOP buying if:**
- Stock up 50%+ in 1 month
- Stock hits all-time high (too extended)
- You've already added 3+ times

**KEEP buying if:**
- Stock on new catalyst announcement
- Stock recovering from dip (retest support)
- Holding during 15-20% pullback

## SPECIFIC EXIT SCENARIOS

### Scenario 1: Stock Down -20% (Stop Hit)

**Action:**
1. EXIT: Sell 100% of position immediately
2. WAIT: Don't buy back for 5 trading days (avoid bottom picking)
3. RE-EVALUATE: Is thesis still intact? Did news break?
4. RE-ENTRY: Only if catalyst is still there and price is lower

**Example:**
- C3.AI drops from $22 to $18 in 2 days
- Hit stop at $18.50, automatically sold
- News: Pentagon contract delayed 6 months
- DECISION: Don't buy back, thesis broken
- MOVE: Redeploy $17,500 to SOUN or QCI instead

### Scenario 2: Stock Up +100% (Should You Sell All?)

**NO, Use This Formula:**

| Total Gain | Sell | Keep |
|-----------|------|------|
| +30% | 25% | 75% |
| +50% | 50% | 50% |
| +100% | 75% | 25% |
| +150% | 90% | 10% |
| +200%+ | 95% | 5% |

**Example:**
- QCI entry: $14
- QCI reaches: $28 (+100%)
- SELL: 75% (450 of 600 shares) = ~$12,600
- KEEP: 25% (150 shares) = potential to $100+
- Reinvest proceeds into weaker positions

### Scenario 3: One Stock Crashes on Bad News

**If company reports negative earnings:**
- SELL immediately (don't wait for stop)
- Take loss if needed
- DO NOT hold and hope
- Redeploy capital to better opportunity

**Example:**
- SOUN reports auto deal fell through
- Stock down 40% in one day
- SELL all remaining position
- MOVE $15,000 to C3.AI or RKLB instead

### Scenario 4: Macro Event (Market Crash)

**If S&P 500 drops 15%+ in one month:**

**Your Options:**
1. **Sell Everything** (lock in gains, sleep at night)
2. **Hold Tight** (these are government-backed, safer than overall market)
3. **Scale In** (markets crash, buy dips with cash)

**Recommended:** Hold core positions (SOUN, C3), use cash to buy dips
