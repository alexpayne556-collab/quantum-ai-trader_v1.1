# SECTOR MOMENTUM HYPOTHESIS

**Date:** December 15, 2025  
**Source:** DAY 4 results discussion  
**Status:** Unvalidated - needs testing

---

## Core Hypothesis

**"If one sector is going, it goes for a while."**

**The edge isn't always finding BEFORE it wins, but finding WHAT'S winning right away in the morning and going with the sectors.**

---

## What This Means

### Traditional Approach (What We've Been Doing):
- Try to predict which stock will move BEFORE it happens
- Volume breakout, pre-event volume, accumulation patterns
- Enter BEFORE the move starts

### Sector Momentum Approach (New Hypothesis):
- Identify which SECTOR is moving RIGHT NOW (morning scan)
- Join the momentum early in the day
- Ride the sector wave (multiple stocks move together)
- Exit when sector momentum fades

---

## Why This Might Work

1. **Sectors move together** - when one stock in a sector moves, others follow
2. **Momentum persists** - sector trends last hours/days, not minutes
3. **Early morning detection** - first 30 minutes shows which sectors are hot
4. **Lower risk** - joining confirmed momentum vs predicting moves
5. **Multiple opportunities** - when a sector moves, many stocks trigger

---

## Examples From Our Data

**Quantum stocks (QUBT, RGTI, IONQ, QMCO):**
- Moved together 28+ times in 12 months
- When QUBT spiked, RGTI followed
- Sector correlation = predictable opportunity

**This validates the hypothesis:**
- Cross-ticker correlations found 25,163 pairs
- Some pairs moved together 20+ times
- These ARE sector plays (quantum, crypto miners, EVs, etc.)

## How To Test This

### Step 1: Define Sectors
Group our 311 tickers into sectors:
- Quantum Computing: QUBT, RGTI, IONQ, QMCO, ARQQ
- Crypto Miners: RIOT, MARA, CIFR, HUT, BTBT, CLSK
- EVs: TSLA, RIVN, LCID, NIO, XPEV
- Biotechs: SANA, CRNC, BLUE, CRSP, EDIT
- Renewables: PLUG, RUN, FCEL, ENPH
- Small caps: Everything else

### Step 2: Morning Scan (9:30-10:00 AM ET)
- Which sector has most stocks moving >2%?
- Which sector has highest average % gain?
- Which sector has volume >3x average?

### Step 3: Entry Rules
- Sector leader: Enter strongest stock in hot sector
- Sector follower: Enter 2nd/3rd strongest stocks (laggards catch up)
- Timing: Enter between 10:00-10:30 AM after initial volatility

### Step 4: Exit Rules
- Profit target: 10% gain OR sector momentum fades
- Stop loss: 5% OR sector turns negative
- Time-based: Exit by 3:00 PM (don't hold overnight)

### Step 5: Backtest
- Re-run DAY4 scanners filtered by sector
- Measure: Do Scanner 1 & 2 perform better when filtered by sector momentum?
- Compare: Sector approach vs single-stock approach

## Expected Results

**If hypothesis is correct:**
- Win rate increases when trading WITH sector momentum
- Average gain increases (catching full sector move, not just one stock)
- Signal frequency increases (multiple stocks per sector)
- Risk decreases (sector confirmation = less false signals)

**If hypothesis is wrong:**
- Sectors don't move together consistently
- Joining momentum = buying tops (late entry)
- Better to predict than react

## Risk Factors

1. **Late entry** - joining momentum = buying after initial move (could be top)
2. **False momentum** - one stock spikes, sector doesn't follow
3. **Whipsaws** - sector reverses quickly, stop loss triggered
4. **Correlation breakdown** - sectors that moved together stop correlating
5. **Sector rotation** - hot sector changes daily, hard to predict
